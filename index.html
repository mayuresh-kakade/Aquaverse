Midnight & abyssal zones · 1,000 m and below

Into the deep

No sunlight reaches here. Pressure rises by about one atmosphere every 10 metres, so at 4,000 m it is roughly 400 times the pressure at the surface. Many animals make their own light, called bioluminescence.

Back toward the light

The ocean needs us, too.

Plastic waste, overfishing, habitat destruction and climate change all stress marine life. Warm water can make corals expel the algae that feed them (bleaching). Recovery is slow: drag the slider to see restoration as a gradual process over years, and only where the causes are addressed.

  • Cut single-use plastic and join local clean-ups.
  • Choose seafood certified as sustainable (e.g. MSC).
  • Use reef-safe habits: don't touch or stand on coral.
  • Lower your carbon footprint — climate change is the biggest reef threat.
  • Support marine protected areas and trusted ocean charities.
  • Dispose of fishing line and nets properly.
